Awarded the AUSOM Faculty Research Grant
chungsoo-kim April 09, 2026 April 22, 2026
notice lab

We are pleased to announce that the Ajou Health Data Science Lab (PI Prof. Chungsoo Kim) has been selected for the New Faculty Research Grant, hosted by the Ajou University School of Medicine (AUSOM). The funded research project is titled “Development of Agentic AI Framework for Medical Observational Research.” Our laboratory will receive full support from the School of Medicine for a period of 1 year to carry out this project. We remain committed to advancing the field of health data science through this opportunity.

Dr Chanyang Min has joined our group!
chungsoo-kim April 01, 2026 April 22, 2026
notice lab

We are thrilled to announce that Dr. Chanyang Min has joined the Ajou Health Data Science Lab as a Research Instructor, effective April 1, 2026. As an expert in nutrition and health data analytics, Dr. Min brings extensive experience in analyzing national health insurance claims data and the Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (KNHANES). Moving forward, Dr. Min will play a key role in generating robust medical evidence through extensive collaborative research with various clinical departments. Please join us in welcoming Dr. Min to the team! 🎉
