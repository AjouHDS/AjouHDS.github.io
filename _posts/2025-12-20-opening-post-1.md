🎄 A joyful update just in time for Christmas! 🎄

I’m happy to share that I’ve been appointed as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Biomedical Informatics at Ajou University as the next step in my academic journey.
I am deeply grateful to the many mentors, collaborators, and colleagues who have supported and encouraged me along the way. I truly could not have reached this milestone without YOU!

At Ajou University, I plan to establish the Ajou Health Data Science Lab, where our research will focus on 
<br>  1) generating reliable evidence from large-scale healthcare data, 
<br>  2) integrating AI and advanced analytics into clinical and population health research, and 
<br>  3) building health data infrastructure that supports reproducible and impactful science.

Through this work, we hope to contribute to improving patient health and clinical decision-making.
Thank you again to everyone who has been part of this journey — and I look forward to what’s ahead!
