From November 13 to 15, 2026, AjouHDS took part in the OHDSI APAC Symposium, held at Yonsei University and Severance Hospital in Sinchon. At the main symposium, Taeseok Yang (MS student) gave a lightning talk titled "Efficient Selection of Negative Control Outcomes via Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent System."
In the two-day datathon that followed, the team of Wonbin Choi, Wooyeol Lee, Taeseok Yang, and Hyuncheol Cho won the 1st Prize with their project "QAIROS: A decision model for drug-induced QT prolongation." Congratulations!

2026년 11월 13일부터 15일까지 연세대학교 및 신촌세브란스병원에서 개최된 OHDSI APAC Symposium에 AjouHDS 연구실이 참여했습니다. Main Symposium에서 양태석 석사과정은 "Efficient Selection of Negative Control Outcomes via Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent System"을 주제로 Lightning talk 발표를 했습니다.
이후 이틀간 진행된 Datathon에 참여한 최원빈, 이우열, 양태석, 조현철 팀은 "QAIROS: A decision model for drug-induced QT prolongation" 프로젝트로 대상(1st Prize)을 수상했습니다. 축하합니다!
