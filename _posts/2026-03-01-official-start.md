We are pleased to announce that as of March 1, 2026, Dr. Chungsoo Kim have officially joined Ajou University as a new faculty member.

Along with this new role, We have officially launched the Ajou Health Data Science Lab within the Department of Biomedical Informatics at the Ajou University School of Medicine.

Our laboratory is located within the department's facilities on the 5th floor of Hongjae Building at Ajou University.
