Prof Chungsoo Kim has recently assumed a new position as an Adjunct Assistant Professor in the Section of Cardiovascular Medicine at Yale School of Medicine and the YNHH Center for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE), effective July 2026. 
Through this position, he will continue to contribute to pharmacoepidemiology and AI research utilizing large-scale healtcare data, especially OMOP and Epic Cosmos, and will serve as a bridge between Korea and the United States, as well as between OHDSI and Yale community. Congratulations.

김청수 교수는 2026년 7월부터 Yale School of Medicine의 Section of Cardiovascular Medicine 및 YNHH Center for Outcomes Research and Evalaution (CORE)의 겸임조교수로의 근무를 새로 시작하게 되었습니다. 
이 position 을 통해서 대규모 의료 데이터를 활용한 약물 역학연구 및 AI연구에 국제적 기여를 계속하게 되며, 한국과 미국, OHDSI와 Yale 간의 교두보 역할을 수행할 것입니다.
